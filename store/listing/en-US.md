## Full description (≤ 4000)

MindKeeper helps you learn something new every day without overload: one short idea at a time, at the time you choose, then a review right before you would forget it.

LEARN ALONG A PATH
• Choose one or more topics.
• Choose a level: Basic, Advanced, Pro or Master.
• Choose how many bites each topic should have.
• AI drafts an outline from easy to hard. Review it, remove items or add your own before you start.

READ FROM THE NOTIFICATION
• Every bite has a short summary: expand the notification to read the key idea without opening the app.
• A "Got it" button on the notification marks it as learned and schedules the review.
• Tap the notification to open exactly that bite.

A CALM HOME SCREEN
• One card tells you what is left today: new bites and review cards, and roughly how long they take.
• "Unread" and "Following" show your progress in each course.

REVIEW AT THE RIGHT TIME WITH FSRS
• Each card has its own schedule from the FSRS spaced-repetition algorithm, running entirely on your device.
• Four recall ratings: Again, Hard, Good, Easy.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads.
• Your knowledge, outlines and review history are stored on your device.
• AI-written content is labelled "Unverified" so you know to double-check it.

BRING YOUR OWN NOTES (OPTIONAL)
• Paste text, import from a link, scan a photo (on-device text recognition), pick a PDF or dictate.

Note: AI-generated content is for study only and is not medical, legal or financial advice.
